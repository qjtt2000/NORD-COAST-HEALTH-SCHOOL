NORD COAST HEALTH INSTITUTE

Career education built around real caregiving skills.

Nord Coast Health Institute was founded in 2025 in Flushing, New York. Our mission is to prepare compassionate, skilled, and dependable caregivers through accessible, industry-aligned training.
